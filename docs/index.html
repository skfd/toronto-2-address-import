Data exploration

City-wide views of the OSM extract and Toronto open-data snapshot used by the import tool. Useful for sanity-checking coverage, multi-addressed buildings, corner cases, and street-name overlaps before scoping a tile.

  • Streets — source vs. OSM street-name overlap (matched / missing / extra).
  • Toronto Open Data ranges — half-numbers and address ranges per street.
  • OSM extract — addresses currently in OSM within the Toronto bbox.
  • Multi-addresses — OSM nodes/ways tagged with more than one address.
  • Corners — corner-lot multi-addresses (two streets).
  • Data — raw datasets behind the pages above.

How the import went

Two views of the campaign as a whole rather than of any one run: a one-page summary of the final figures, and a replay of the operator's work spreading across the city tile by tile.

  • Wrap-up — the campaign in one page: daily uploads, hands-on time, conflation funnel, top streets and neighbourhoods, automation split. Generated from the tool database, so it stays current as monthly maintenance accumulates.
  • Operator activity — a scrubable replay of every review and upload across the 1,297 tiles, with idle gaps compressed.
  • Wrap-up (closing day) — the original hand-made one-pager, kept as a snapshot of the figures on 28 May 2026.
